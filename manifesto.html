This is the future of Mumbai. And every home and city in the world.

Cities are our greatest invention. They bring people together, create opportunity. They concentrate knowledge, culture and enterprise. This is why Mumbai is what it is, the city that never sleeps.

The House of Mittal Builders had a substantial role in making this city what it is. But the assumptions we built our cities on yesterday, no longer hold any more. Energy won’t flow ceaselessly and abundantly, nor will water. Our climate is not stable and land, unfortunately, cannot be manufactured. This is the new reality of our cities. And Mumbai is entering this chapter.

Mumbai has more people coming in every day. Adding more pressure on water, energy and infrastructure. This raises a lot of questions.

The answer is not to build less. The answer is to build differently.

To build efficiently and resiliently. At Urna we believe sustainability is not an amenity. Or a marketing claim. Or an option. It is the foundation of responsible urban development.

At Urna we believe every building should give more than it takes. More energy resilience. More water security. More biodiversity. More community. More value over time. Mumbai’s future should not be a compromise, it should definitely be an improvement.

Buildings are not isolated objects. They are living parts of a larger ecosystem; buildings and homes should participate in the life around them. Grow food. Harvest water. Generate energy. Clean air. Strengthen community.

This is the future Urna is building.

One project, one home, one community, one neighbourhood at a time — we are building Mumbai’s tomorrow.
